
# 🩺 Smart Doctor – Telemedicine & Appointment Management Platform

> A robust, modern full-stack telemedicine and appointment management platform designed to simplify interactions between patients and healthcare providers, ensuring accessible and efficient digital healthcare services.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The healthcare industry is undergoing a rapid digital transformation to meet the growing demand for accessible medical services. **Smart Doctor** solves key challenges in healthcare administration by offering seamless appointment scheduling, secure user authentication, and an integrated video-consultation framework. Built on a modular architecture, it minimizes administrative overhead while ensuring high scalability and performance.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* 🗓️ **Real-Time Availability Tracking:** Prevents overlapping bookings and manages real-time slot scheduling.
* 🔍 **Specialization-Based Search:** Responsive doctor search and filtering module based on specializations and qualifications.
* 📹 **Remote Video Consultation:** Integrated framework for secure, remote patient-doctor consultations.
* 🔐 **Secure Authentication & Onboarding:** Powered by Clerk for safe authentication and streamlined doctor onboarding processes.
* 🤖 **AI Symptom Analyzer (Upcoming):** Preliminary AI-driven symptom analysis to provide instant guidance when healthcare providers are unavailable.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** [Next.js](https://nextjs.org/)
* **Backend & ORM:** [Prisma ORM](https://www.prisma.io/)
* **Database:** [PostgreSQL](https://www.postgresql.org/)
* **Authentication:** [Clerk](https://clerk.com/)
